Agent Tier List

Patch 13.09 Updated: Nov 2026

Current competitive meta rankings based on pro play, high-elo win rates & ranked data (Act VI).

SMeta Defining AStrong BViable CNiche / Map Specific DStruggling

Duelist Meta

Neon defines the meta on Pearl/Fracture/Lotus. Jett & Raze remain elite entry. Waylay rising with vertical mobility. Yoru/Reyna situational. Phoenix struggles.

Initiator Meta

Sova still king of info. Skye/Fade flex for flash/heal. KAY/O anti-meta vs util comps. Tejo/Gekko map-specific. Breach niche execute tool.

Controller Meta

Viper mandatory Bind/Breeze/Icebox. Omen universal flex. Harbor execute king on Lotus/Pearl. Astra high-ceiling. Brimstone simple but limited. Miks unproven.

Sentinel Meta

Cypher/Killjoy anchor standard. Clove S-tier flex (post-death value). Deadlock/Vyse anti-entry niche. Chamber fallen. Sage support only. Veto unproven.
